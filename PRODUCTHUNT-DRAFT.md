# Product Hunt Launch Draft — RetroTV.fun

## Title
RetroTV.fun

## Tagline
A daily TV show guessing game for 70s & 80s fans — like Wordle meets classic TV

## Description
Remember rushing home to catch your favorite show? RetroTV.fun brings back that nostalgia with a daily guessing game for classic TV lovers.

Every day, you get a new screenshot from a 70s or 80s TV show. Guess the show in as few tries as possible. Each wrong guess reveals another frame as a hint.

**Why you'll love it:**
- 🎯 New puzzle every day — one show, multiple frames
- 📺 200+ classic TV shows from the golden age of television
- 🔓 No login, no signup, no app to install — just play
- 📊 Track your streak and share your results
- 🧠 Perfect for fans of Wordle, Framed, and daily puzzle games

Whether you grew up with M*A*S*H, The A-Team, or Knight Rider, RetroTV.fun will test your TV knowledge and take you on a trip down memory lane.

**Free forever. Play at [RetroTV.fun](https://retrotv.fun)**

## First Comment (from maker)
Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I built RetroTV.fun because I'm obsessed with 70s and 80s TV and wanted a daily brain teaser that scratched that nostalgia itch. Think Framed (framed.wtf) but specifically for classic television.

The game pulls frames from 200+ shows and presents a new puzzle every day. I've been amazed at how much people remember (or think they remember!) from shows they haven't seen in decades.

It's completely free, no account needed — just open the site and start guessing. Would love to hear what you think, and whether you can beat my streak! 📺
